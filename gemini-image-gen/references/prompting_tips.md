# Gemini Prompting Tips & Best Practices

Detailed guidance for getting the most out of Gemini 2.5 Flash and Gemini 3 Pro (Nano Banana Pro).

## Elements of a High-Quality Prompt

To achieve professional results, include these five core elements in your vision:

*   **Subject**: Be specific (e.g., "a stoic robot barista with glowing blue optics").
*   **Composition**: Define the framing (e.g., "extreme close-up," "cinematic 21:9 wide shot," "low angle").
*   **Action**: Describe what is happening (e.g., "mid-stride running through a field").
*   **Location**: Set the scene (e.g., "a futuristic cafe on Mars").
*   **Style**: Specify the aesthetic (e.g., "3D animation," "film noir," "watercolor painting").

## Advanced Cinematography & Lighting

Move beyond basics by directing the shot like a cinematographer:

*   **Camera Settings**: Use terms like "shallow depth of field (f/1.8)" or "low-angle shot."
*   **Lighting**: Specify "golden hour backlighting," "long shadows," or "neon-drenched nights."
*   **Color Grading**: Suggest palettes like "muted teal tones" or "vibrant 1970s retro colors."

## Text Integration & Rendering

Gemini excels at rendering legible text. To use this effectively:

*   **Be Explicit**: State exactly what text should appear.
*   **Font Styling**: Describe the font (e.g., "bold, white, sans-serif font at the top").
*   **Context**: "An infographic explaining photosynthesis" or "A movie poster with the title 'STELAR'."

## Multi-Image Reference Strategy

When using multiple images (up to 14 in Gemini 3 Pro), define their roles clearly:

*   **Role Assignment**: "Use Image A for the character's pose, Image B for the art style, and Image C for the background."
*   **Consistency**: Mention "Maintain character consistency across different scenes."

## Specialized Workflows

*   **Draping & Branding**: Design apparel or packaging by "draping" patterns onto 3D objects.
*   **Translation & Localization**: "Translate all English text on these cans into Korean while keeping the design identical."
*   **Storyboarding**: "Create a 4-panel storyboard for this scene showing establishing shot, close-up, and POV."

## Editing Instructions

When modifying images, be direct and surgical:
*   "Change the man's tie to emerald green."
*   "Remove the car in the background and fill the space with trees."
*   "Turn this daytime scene into a rainy night."
